Analytic sky initialization and evaluation (Apache-2.0):
Copyright 2011-2022 Blender Foundation.
Adapted from intern/cycles/scene/shader_nodes.cpp and kernel/svm/sky.h.
vibrt's sky_bridge.cpp replaces Cycles scene/kernel dependencies with an owned
CPU evaluator, Rec.709 conversion, image filtering and a Rust C ABI.

The original model sources are unchanged. The standalone math, scheduling and
declaration headers are supplied by vibrt (MIT); Blender's GPL helper headers
are not bundled. Full license texts are included beside this notice.

Cycles shader mathematics (Blender v5.1.1):
https://github.com/blender/blender/tree/v5.1.1/intern/cycles
Copyright 2011-2022 Blender Foundation and Blender Authors (Apache-2.0).
The standalone adapter extracts numerical helpers and replaces SVM stack,
kernel globals, image access and shading context with vibrt's CPU/CUDA ABI.
Noise, fractals, Voronoi, Gabor, Wave, Brick, Checker, Gradient, Magic,
color/vector math, radial tiling, image projection, normal maps, Fresnel
and tangent evaluation use or adapt these sources. Surface closures also reuse
Oren-Nayar, Sheen LTC, Ashikhmin Velvet, microfacet evaluation/sampling,
conductor/generalized Fresnel and thin-film/energy tables (Apache-2.0).
Source bytes are pinned by the Blender git submodule at
vibrt-nodes/third_party/blender, commit b70da489d7f4eae7760cc0a50cc6c60cd5464205.
Provenance and adapter details are in vibrt-nodes/third_party/cycles/README.md.

Subsurface scattering uses the Cycles Burley profiles, random-walk coefficient
fit and dipole radius fit (Apache-2.0). The renderer supplies BVH traversal and
free-flight sampling. Pointiness is adapted from intern/cycles/blender/mesh.cpp
(Apache-2.0); vibrt/src/pointiness.rs supplies the welded mesh attribute.

Additional Cycles kernels (Apache-2.0, Blender Foundation/Blender Authors):
Huang hair, light sampling/spread and light-tree SAOH construction, image
interpolation, camera apertures, film filter CDFs, compact differentials,
SVM bump/normal-map/wireframe, Bevel profiles, adaptive sampling, shadow
transparency and equiangular volume sampling. The adapters replace Blender
scene storage and kernel globals; the pinned submodule is not modified.
